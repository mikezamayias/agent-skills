## Transferable Observations

- **1:05–1:40:** Existing paid competitors provide demand evidence. Differentiation can be narrower than invention of a new category.
- **1:47–2:27:** Scope MVP around one feature and one satisfying, demonstrable outcome.
- **2:33–3:37:** Ship early for real feedback. Keep high standards for sensitive code while avoiding premature scale work.
- **5:48–6:09:** Instrument onboarding, trial, and paid conversion so product decisions use observed behavior.
- **6:35–6:55:** Generate several creative directions before paying for production.
- **11:37–12:18:** After launch, prioritize marketing unless bugs, specific requests, or measured blockers justify product work.
- **12:25–13:12:** Compare active creator outreach with intent-based paid acquisition and learn from each channel separately.

## Case-Study Numbers, Not Benchmarks

Source reports roughly:

- App shipped in under two weeks.
- More than $500 revenue in first month.
- About 50 creator DMs per day.
- Less than 10% of respondents progressing to a post.
- Several hundred dollars of Apple Search Ads producing roughly one or two paid trials at that point.

These numbers show volume, attribution, and profitability concerns. Never reuse them as universal targets.

## Adaptation Choices

- Replace named coding, design, analytics, and creative tools with capability requirements.
- Expand money path through refunds and retention because revenue alone is incomplete.
- Add explicit spend caps, attribution checks, and unit-economics gates.
- Separate reversible experiments from auth, payment, privacy, secret, and destructive paths.
